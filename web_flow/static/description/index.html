Web Flow: a Canvas Widget for Flows of Steps

Web Flow draws a tree of steps with branches on a canvas, like the campaigns of a marketing automation, for any model of your modules, on Odoo Community and Enterprise. Users add a step where it goes with the + buttons, edit it in place, move it, zoom and pan.

Two mixins
web.flow.mixin for the flow, web.flow.node.mixin for its steps, with the tree rules built in.
One widget
widget="web_flow" on the one2many of the steps: cards, curved connectors, branch labels, END of the branches.
Run monitors
A status per step (done, running, waiting, failed) to follow the runs of a flow on the same canvas.

Screenshot

Use it in your module

  1. The flow inherits web.flow.mixin; its steps inherit web.flow.node.mixin, with a many2one to the flow (_flow_container_field, flow_id by default), a parent_id to the previous step and a type field (_flow_type_field, node_type by default).
  2. The steps define _flow_node_types() (the types offered by the + buttons, with icon, help and category), _flow_branch_list() (the branches under a step) and optionally _flow_card_data() (summary and warning of the card).
  3. In the form of the flow, put widget="web_flow" on the one2many of the steps.
class MyFlowStep(models.Model):
    _name = 'my.flow.step'
    _inherit = ['web.flow.node.mixin']

    flow_id = fields.Many2one('my.flow', required=True, ondelete='cascade')
    parent_id = fields.Many2one('my.flow.step', ondelete='cascade')
    node_type = fields.Selection([('task', 'Task'), ('check', 'Check')], required=True)

    @api.model
    def _flow_node_types(self):
        return [{'type': 'task', 'label': 'Task', 'icon': 'checklist', 'help': 'Do something',
                 'category': 'Work', 'shape': 'card'},
                {'type': 'check', 'label': 'Check', 'icon': 'fork_right', 'help': 'Yes or No',
                 'category': 'Rules', 'shape': 'card'}]

    def _flow_branch_list(self):
        return [('yes', False), ('no', False)] if self.node_type == 'check' else [('next', False)]

Features

  • Insert a step above another, delete a step and its branch, re-layout.
  • Drag the cards anywhere: their position is kept.
  • Pan, zoom (Ctrl + wheel), fit to view, full screen; option fill to use the height left in the page.
  • Loops are not drawn: a step type of your model can lead to another step (e.g. "Go to step").

Flow view

A view type flow for any model: the records as the cards of a kanban view, from left to right in horizontal lanes by the group by (split in sub-lanes by a second group by), with the connectors of the links between them.

<flow default_group_by="stage_id" type_field="flow_type_id" card_id="...">
    <link field="depend_on_ids" string="Blocked by"/>
    <link field="parent_id" string="Sub-task" line="dashed" arrow="0" route="orthogonal"/>
</flow>
  • Links on many2one or many2many fields to the model itself, listing the records before (default) or after (direction="next"); line solid, dashed or dotted, arrow, color, curved or orthogonal connectors.
  • A record is placed after the records before it, on their row or below; the connectors go from the right side of a card to the left side of the next one and never over a card.
  • Hover a card: the records before and after it and their connectors stand out. Click a link in the legend to hide or show it.
  • Hover a connector and click its + to insert a record of a type (type_field, grouped by category, with icons) between the two records.
  • Lanes and sub-lanes with their names written vertically, folded on a click; drag a card to another lane to change its values; the records after a record can be folded.
