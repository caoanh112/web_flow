Web Flow: Flow View and Canvas Widget for Odoo

Web Flow shows how your records follow each other. Its flow view draws the records of any model from left to right in lanes, with the links between them (blocked by, parent, next...), and its canvas widget draws and edits a tree of steps with branches, like the campaigns of a marketing automation. On Odoo Community and Enterprise.

Flow view
A view type flow: the kanban cards of your records in lanes by stage (or any group by), joined by the connectors of their links.
Canvas widget
widget="web_flow" on a one2many of steps: cards, curved connectors, branch labels, + buttons, zoom and pan.
Run monitors
A status per step (done, running, waiting, failed) to follow the runs of a flow on the same canvas.

Flow view

The tasks of a project as a flow: lanes by stage, the tasks after the tasks that block them, the sub-tasks next to their task.

Hover a task: what it waits for and what waits for it stand out.

Hover a connector and click +: insert a record of a type between two records.

Declare it in a view

<record id="project_task_view_flow" model="ir.ui.view">
    <field name="model">project.task</field>
    <field name="arch" type="xml">
        <flow default_group_by="stage_id" type_field="flow_type_id" card_id="%(project.view_task_card)d">
            <link field="depend_on_ids" string="Blocked by"/>
            <link field="parent_id" string="Sub-task" line="dashed" arrow="0" route="orthogonal"/>
        </flow>
    </field>
</record>

Features

  • Links on many2one or many2many fields to the model itself, listing the records before (default) or after (direction="next"); line solid, dashed or dotted, arrow, color, curved or orthogonal connectors; a legend to hide or show each link.
  • A record is placed after the records before it, on their row or below; the connectors go from the right side of a card to the left side of the next one and never over a card.
  • The cards are the cards of your kanban view (card_id or a card template), all of the same size.
  • Lanes by the group by of the search, sub-lanes by a second group by, their names written vertically; fold a lane on a click; drag a card to another lane to change its values.
  • A + on each connector inserts a record of a type (type_field, grouped by category, with icons) between the two records; the records after a record can be folded.

Canvas widget

The canvas as used by AI Workflow: steps of different types, Yes / No branches, + buttons.

Use it in your module

  1. The flow inherits web.flow.mixin; its steps inherit web.flow.node.mixin, with a many2one to the flow (_flow_container_field, flow_id by default), a parent_id to the previous step and a type field (_flow_type_field, node_type by default).
  2. The steps define _flow_node_types() (the types offered by the + buttons, with icon, help and category), _flow_branch_list() (the branches under a step) and optionally _flow_card_data() (summary and warning of the card).
  3. In the form of the flow, put widget="web_flow" on the one2many of the steps.
class MyFlowStep(models.Model):
    _name = 'my.flow.step'
    _inherit = ['web.flow.node.mixin']

    flow_id = fields.Many2one('my.flow', required=True, ondelete='cascade')
    parent_id = fields.Many2one('my.flow.step', ondelete='cascade')
    node_type = fields.Selection([('task', 'Task'), ('check', 'Check')], required=True)

    @api.model
    def _flow_node_types(self):
        return [{'type': 'task', 'label': 'Task', 'icon': 'checklist', 'help': 'Do something',
                 'category': 'Work', 'shape': 'card'},
                {'type': 'check', 'label': 'Check', 'icon': 'fork_right', 'help': 'Yes or No',
                 'category': 'Rules', 'shape': 'card'}]

    def _flow_branch_list(self):
        return [('yes', False), ('no', False)] if self.node_type == 'check' else [('next', False)]

Features

  • Insert a step above another, delete a step and its branch, re-layout.
  • Drag the cards anywhere: their position is kept.
  • Pan, zoom (Ctrl + wheel), fit to view, full screen; option fill to use the height left in the page.
  • Loops are not drawn: a step type of your model can lead to another step (e.g. "Go to step").
